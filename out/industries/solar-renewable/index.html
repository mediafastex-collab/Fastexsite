The problem

Residential tactics do not work on a commercial capex decision.

Most solar marketing is built for homeowners — form fills, price comparisons, quick quotes. A C&I project runs into crores, involves three or four stakeholders, and takes months. Buying the same lead lists as everyone else produces enquiries your team wastes weeks disqualifying.

  • Shared aggregator leads that three competitors are calling the same morning
  • Enquiries from tenants and office managers with no authority over the roof or the budget
  • No material that answers the questions a CFO actually asks about payback and financing
  • Deals that stall after the site survey because nothing kept the conversation warm

Questions

Before you book the call.

Do you generate residential solar leads?+

No. We work only on commercial and industrial projects. Residential solar is a volume game won on price and speed of quotation; C&I is a relationship sale to a facility or finance decision-maker. Those need opposite systems, and we are built for the second.

Our sales cycle is six to nine months. Does outbound still make sense?+

It makes more sense, not less. Long cycles punish inconsistent prospecting — a quiet quarter of outreach becomes a dead quarter of revenue three quarters later. We keep the top of the funnel filled continuously so your pipeline stays predictable across the cycle.

Is WhatsApp outreach compliant?+

We run permission-based sequences through approved business messaging with clear opt-outs, and we do not scrape numbers or blast unverified lists. The goal is a warm conversation with a decision-maker, and that only happens when the first message is relevant and welcome.

Can you handle multiple states or service regions?+

Yes. We segment lists and campaigns by geography so each region gets messaging matched to its tariff structure, subsidy position and industrial mix. Reporting is broken down the same way, so you can see which belts are worth expanding into.

Who takes the meetings you book?+

Your sales engineers. We qualify for roof or land availability, electricity spend and decision authority before a meeting is confirmed, so your technical team spends time on sites worth surveying rather than on enquiries that were never viable.
