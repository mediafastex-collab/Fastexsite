The problem

Founder-led sales stops scaling long before the pipeline does.

Most software companies hit the same wall. The founder closes well, referrals dry up, and the team starts spraying generic sequences at a list bought from a database. Technical buyers spot it immediately. The result is a pipeline that looks busy and a calendar that stays empty.

  • Lists built from job titles alone, with no read on tech stack, funding stage or hiring signals
  • Messaging written for a marketing persona, sent to an engineer who wants specifics
  • Domains burned by volume sending, so even the good messages never reach the inbox
  • No follow-up system, so a warm reply dies because nobody chased it on the third channel

Questions

Before you book the call.

Do you work with early-stage SaaS companies?+

Yes. We work best with software companies that have found product-market fit and closed at least a handful of customers, because we need real win stories to build messaging from. If you are pre-revenue and still testing the problem, outbound will amplify a message you have not validated yet — we will tell you that on the call rather than take the engagement.

How long before we see the first qualified meeting?+

Weeks one and two go to ICP research, domain setup and inbox warm-up — sending hard before that burns the domain. Sequences typically go live in week three, with first replies in the same week and consistent meeting flow by weeks six to eight. Anyone promising meetings in week one is sending from a domain they are willing to lose.

How do you keep our main domain safe?+

We never send volume from your primary domain. We buy and configure separate sending domains with SPF, DKIM and DMARC, warm the inboxes gradually, and cap daily volume per mailbox. Your corporate domain keeps its reputation for the mail your team actually sends.

Can you sell a technical product without knowing the technology?+

We do not pretend to be engineers. We run structured onboarding with your technical team and your best sales calls, and we write from the specifics that come out of that — the integration that saves three weeks, the compliance requirement you already clear. Vague benefit language is what gets ignored by this audience.

Do you replace our sales team?+

No. We fill the calendar; your team runs the calls and closes. Most clients pair us with one or two account executives. If you have nobody to take the meetings, more meetings will not help you.
