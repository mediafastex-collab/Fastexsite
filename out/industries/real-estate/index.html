Questions

Before you book the call.

Do you generate residential home-buyer leads?+

Only where the sale is genuinely B2B or high-ticket investment — bulk buyers, investor syndicates, overseas investor portfolios, channel partners. Mass residential lead generation is a volume-and-price game run through portals, and other agencies do it more cheaply than we would.

How is this different from buying portal leads?+

Portal leads are shared, inbound and priced per enquiry — you compete with three others on response speed. Outbound gives you exclusive conversations with buyers and partners who were not shopping that morning, and the relationship belongs to you rather than to the platform.

Can you help us recruit channel partners in a new city?+

Yes, and it is usually the fastest way into a new micro-market. We map active brokers and partners in the territory, verify they transact in your ticket range, and run sequences around commission, inventory access and support. Your team then handles the onboarding conversation.

Our sales cycle runs six months or more. How do you keep leads warm?+

With structured multi-touch follow-up rather than hope. Prospects move into WhatsApp and email nurture sequences with construction updates, comparable transactions and yield data, so when the decision window opens your project is the one still in the conversation.

Who handles the site visits?+

Your sales team. We qualify on requirement, budget, timeline and decision authority before confirming a meeting, so your team spends its time on visits with a genuine transaction behind them.
