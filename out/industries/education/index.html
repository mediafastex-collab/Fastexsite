The problem

Admissions marketing and institutional growth are different jobs.

Most institutes run one marketing function aimed at students and parents, then wonder why corporate tie-ups, placement partnerships and franchise expansion never move. Those are B2B sales into organisations with committees, procurement and budget cycles — and they need their own system.

  • Placement and partnership targets owned by someone with no pipeline to work from
  • EdTech products pitched to teachers when the budget sits with a trustee or director
  • Franchise and campus expansion dependent on inbound enquiries and personal networks
  • Admission enquiries arriving in volume but unqualified, burning the counselling team

Questions

Before you book the call.

We are a college, not a company. Does B2B outbound apply to us?+

It applies to everything you sell to an organisation rather than to a student — corporate placement partnerships, training mandates, research collaborations, campus recruitment drives and franchise expansion. Those are institutional sales with committees and approval cycles, and they respond to structured outreach.

Can you help with student admissions directly?+

We help with the system around it: qualified enquiry generation, and WhatsApp follow-up through the decision window. We are not a substitute for your counselling team, and we will not promise enrolment numbers — the counsellor conversation decides that, and we do not run it.

Our budget cycle is tied to the academic year. How do you plan around it?+

We map sequences backwards from your buyers' cycles. For institutional sales that means reaching decision-makers before budgets are locked, typically one to two quarters ahead. For placement partnerships it means engaging recruiters well ahead of hiring season rather than during it.

Will outreach damage our institutional reputation?+

It would, if it were volume spam — which is why we do not run that. Sequences go out from properly configured domains, in low daily volumes, written as a specific and relevant approach from your institution. Your name is the asset being used, and protecting it is a constraint we design around.

Do you work with EdTech companies selling to institutes?+

Yes, and it is one of our stronger fits. The sale involves multiple stakeholders, a real implementation question and a budget cycle — exactly the conditions where structured multi-channel outbound outperforms paid advertising.
