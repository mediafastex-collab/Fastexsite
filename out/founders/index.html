01 · Founder Personal Branding

Krupa Dalal

The brief

Krupa Dalal came to us to build a personal brand on LinkedIn: one that reaches the right audience and shows clearly what Krupa stands for as a founder.

What we built

A clear position on what the founder stands for, a LinkedIn profile rewritten to say it, and a steady stream of posts drafted from conversations with them and written in the founder's own voice.
