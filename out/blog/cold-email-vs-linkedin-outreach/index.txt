<h2>The Case for Cold Email</h2>
<p><strong>Scale.</strong> With proper domain infrastructure you can reach thousands of qualified prospects monthly. No platform caps.</p>
<p><strong>Cost.</strong> Per contact cost is the lowest of any outbound channel, period.</p>
<p><strong>Automation depth.</strong> Multi step sequences, A/B testing and AI personalisation run on autopilot while you sleep.</p>
<p>The catch? Deliverability is a discipline. Without warmed domains, verified lists and spam tested copy, campaigns die silently in junk folders. This setup work is exactly why "we tried cold email, it didn't work" usually means "we skipped the infrastructure".</p>

<h2>The Case for LinkedIn Outreach</h2>
<p><strong>Trust.</strong> Your profile, mutual connections and content history vouch for you before you say a word.</p>
<p><strong>Reply quality.</strong> Conversations start warmer. Decision makers reply as themselves, not through gatekeepers.</p>
<p><strong>Authority compounding.</strong> Every post you publish makes every future message land better. For agencies like <strong>Astronuts</strong> and <strong>The Creative Co</strong>, our LinkedIn engine books meetings because targeting and credibility work together.</p>
<p>The catch? Hard limits of roughly <strong>20 to 25 connection requests per day</strong> mean LinkedIn alone can't feed a hungry pipeline. And without a credible profile, outreach falls flat.</p>

<img src="https://www.fastexmedia.com/assets/blog/cold-email-vs-linkedin-outreach-2.png" alt="Outbound Strategy key statistic" loading="lazy">

<h2>Head to Head Numbers</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Reply rate:</strong> LinkedIn 10 to 25% · Email 3 to 8%</li>
<li><strong>Monthly reach:</strong> LinkedIn around 500 to 600 new contacts · Email thousands with multiple domains</li>
<li><strong>Setup time:</strong> LinkedIn days · Email 2 to 3 weeks of domain warmup</li>
<li><strong>Best for:</strong> LinkedIn suits high ticket, relationship led sales · Email suits volume pipeline and testing</li>
</ul>

<h2>Why Together Beats Either</h2>
<p>The highest performing system we run sequences both. Email opens the door at scale. LinkedIn adds a face and credibility. A WhatsApp follow up converts warm interest into booked calls.</p>
<p>Prospects touched on two channels reply at roughly <strong>2x the rate</strong> of single channel outreach. For <strong>QuickEst CRM</strong>, LinkedIn layered over email sequences turned a cold list into a predictable qualified pipeline.</p>

<h2>Which Should You Start With?</h2>
<p><strong>Start with LinkedIn if</strong> your deals are high ticket, your founder has a credible profile, and you need conversations this month.</p>
<p><strong>Start with email if</strong> you need volume, your prospect list is large, and you can invest 2 to 3 weeks in proper setup.</p>
<p><strong>Do both if</strong> you're serious about predictable pipeline. That's exactly the system we build.</p>


            <h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
                <h3>Which has better reply rates, cold email or LinkedIn?</h3>
                <p>LinkedIn wins per message: 10 to 25% replies versus 3 to 8% for cold email. Your profile adds credibility and the inbox is less crowded. But email scales far past LinkedIn's daily limits, so total pipeline volume often favours email.</p>
                <h3>Is cold email still effective in 2026?</h3>
                <p>Yes, when the infrastructure is right. Warmed domains, verified lists, and genuine personalisation still book meetings at predictable cost. Generic blasts die in spam. When someone says cold email doesn't work, they usually skipped the setup.</p>
                <h3>Can I run LinkedIn outreach and cold email together?</h3>
                <p>You should. Prospects who see you on both channels reply at roughly double the rate. Email brings scale, LinkedIn brings trust. They cover each other's blind spots.</p>
                <h3>How many messages can I send per day?</h3>
                <p>Email: 30 to 50 per sending domain per day, so scaling means multiple warmed domains. LinkedIn: around 20 to 25 connection requests daily on a warmed account.
